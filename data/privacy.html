Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

Bible Reels Studio accesses social media accounts solely via official OAuth 2.0 authorization granted by the user. We store access tokens locally to allow publishing requested media.

2. How We Use Information

We use account permissions solely to upload and publish videos and captions explicitly requested by the creator.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or user credentials to third parties.

4. Token Revocation

You can revoke access tokens at any time through your TikTok account settings or by deleting local session data.

5. Contact

For questions regarding your privacy, please contact the administrator.
