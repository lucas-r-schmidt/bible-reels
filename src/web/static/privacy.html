Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

BibleReels ("we", "our") respects your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle data when you use our scheduling services.

1. Information We Collect

When you authorize BibleReels via TikTok OAuth, we only receive necessary tokens and basic profile information (such as username and user ID) to perform video uploads and publishing on your behalf.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information solely to schedule and publish the videos you have approved. We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third-party advertisers.

3. Data Retention and Security

Authentication tokens are stored securely in your private environment and are not shared across accounts.

4. User Rights and Data Deletion

You can revoke access at any time through TikTok's security settings or by deleting your connection credentials in BibleReels.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@biblereels.local
